Assured Safety, Security & Intent with Systematic Tactics

The ASSIST Research Lab was established in support of the Harris Institute of Assured Information at the Florida Institute of Technology to advance innovative research in developing assurance technologies. The ASSIST Research Lab is directed by Dr. Siddhartha Bhattacharyya. The research focuses on the application of theories to practice. As a result, the research involves collaborative efforts with industry, academic, and national lab partners. Some of the present projects are related to providing assurance for Autonomous Systems, Avionics, Internet of Things, Communication Systems, Cyber Security, and Systems Biology.

02 / CORE DOMAINS

ASSIST (Assured Safety, Security and Intent with Systematic Tactics) Research Lab focuses on the design and development of assurance frameworks for mission-critical, safety-critical, and security-critical systems. The assurance frameworks designed integrate tools based on theories of computation, formal methods, model-based engineering, architecture analysis, and software/systems engineering principles.

Technical Domains
DOMAIN 01

Formal Methods

Formal methods for safety-critical systems, model-based assurance frameworks, formal verification of deep neural networks, and cognitive modeling for human-machine interactions.

DOMAIN 02

Machine Learning Engineering

Assured machine learning for safety-critical systems, adversarial robustness, distribution shift detection, and model-based assurance frameworks for deep neural networks.

DOMAIN 03

Trustworthy AI/ML

Trustworthy AI/ML for autonomous systems, assurance of deep neural networks, adversarial robustness, and model-based assurance frameworks for safety-critical systems.

DOMAIN 04

Cognitive modeling

Cognitive modeling for human-machine interactions, model-based assurance frameworks for safety-critical systems, and formal verification of deep neural networks.

DOMAIN 05

Model based Engineering

Model-based engineering for safety-critical systems, assurance of deep neural networks, adversarial robustness, and model-based assurance frameworks for autonomous systems.
